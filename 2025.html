2025 TRAVEL CHRONICLES

Exploring the diverse culture and architecture across major cities in Asia.

January 23~27, 2025

Historical Shrines of Kyoto

Walking down traditional alleys at dawn. The crisp winter air combined with the sight of preserved wooden machiya architecture brings a deep sense of historical continuity. / 黎明時分漫步於傳統小徑。清冽的冬夜空氣與完整保存的木造町家建築相輝映，帶來一種強烈的歷史延續感。

July 01~22, 2025

The Bund and Modern Skyscrapers

Witnessing the stunning contrast between European historic facades on one side and futuristic neon towers on the other across the Huangpu River. / 目睹黃浦江兩岸的震撼對比：一側是充滿歷史底蘊的歐式建築群，另一側則是科技感十足的未來派摩天大樓。

December 20~25, 2025

Vibrant Night Markets & Taipei 101

Savoring hot traditional street food under the glittering city lights. The dynamic pulse of the night market stands as a perfect conclusion to the 2025 journey. / 在閃爍的城市燈火下細細品嚐熱騰騰的傳統街頭小吃。夜市裡充滿生命力的律動，為這趟 2025 年的旅程畫下了最完美的句點。
