Balancing the rigorous pursuit of laboratory research with a profound fascination for regional histories, I use this travel log as a digital canvas. My academic training teaches me to observe microscopic mechanisms closely, while traveling inspires me to capture macroeconomic, cultural connections across major Asian cities. / 平衡實驗室研究的嚴謹追求與對區域歷史的深厚著迷，我將這份旅遊日誌視為數位畫布。我的學術訓練教會我近距離觀察微觀機制，而旅行則啟發我捕捉亞洲各大城市宏觀的文化連結。

Every photograph taken and journal entry compiled serves as an exploration of structural beauty, whether found in a complex biological pathway or ancient temple architecture. Through this platform, I document the fluid evolution of modern cities like Osaka, Shanghai, and Taipei. / 每張拍攝的照片與彙編的日誌條目，都是對結構美學的探索，無論是在複雜的生物通路中，還是古老的寺廟建築裡。透過這個平台，我記錄了大阪、上海和台北等現代城市的流動演變。

Documenting the rich juxtaposition of local night market ecosystem with global landmarks like Taipei 101, gathering cultural data and completing the 2025 travel chronicle trilogy. / 記錄在地夜市生態系統與台北 101 等國際地標的豐富並存，收集人文素材並正式完成 2025 年城市旅遊紀實三部曲。
