JAPAN CHRONICLES 2025 / 日本行旅紀錄

Exploring the extremes of seasons: From mountain snowscapes to autumn leaves

JANUARY 10, 2025

Dawn Over Frozen Kawaguchiko / 富士山與河口湖冬曉

Waking up at 5:00 AM in sub-zero temperatures. Watching the first light hit Mount Fuji while ice floats along the lake edges is an unforgettable, peaceful experience. / 清晨五點在零下低溫中醒來。看著第一道曙光灑落於富士山頭，伴隨著湖畔碎冰折射的光芒，是一段無比寧靜且難忘的感官體驗。

JANUARY 15, 2025

Neon Rain Cascades in Shinjuku / 新宿雨夜的霓虹幻影

A sudden freezing winter drizzle turned the narrow pathways of Tokyo into neon-lit mirrors. Found shelter in a hidden ramen shop tucked behind the main streets. / 一場突如其來的冰冷冬雨，將東京狹窄的巷弄轉化為流動的霓虹鏡面。轉身躲進主幹道後方隱密的小拉麵店，享受著熱氣騰騰的歸屬感。

JANUARY 22, 2025

The Snow Gardens of Kinkaku-ji / 金閣寺的鋪雪金芒

Witnessing Kyoto's iconic Golden Pavilion surrounded by fresh, white snow. The contrast between the brilliant gold leaf and the pristine winter landscape is breathtaking. / 有幸目睹京都著名的金閣寺披上一層無瑕的白雪。耀眼的黃金葉片建築與周圍純淨雪景產生的強烈對比，美得令人屏息、難以忘懷。

DECEMBER 03, 2025

Crimson Canopies of Eikando / 永觀堂的楓紅漫天

Late autumn momiji leaves turning a deep vermilion shade over ancient wooden pathways. The autumn foliage season here creates a breathtaking natural roof. / 季末的紅葉在古老的木造迴廊上方交織成深硃砂色的天幕。深秋時節的楓林在頭頂形成了一片極具震撼力的自然穹頂。

DECEMBER 07, 2025

Twilight Shadows in Bamboo Groves / 嵐山竹林的暮色幽徑

Walking down Arashiyama's pathways as twilight sets in. The gentle rustle of towering green bamboo stalks against the cool winter breeze feels mystical. / 在日落暮色低垂之際漫步於嵐山竹林小徑。高聳入天的翠綠竹節隨清冷冬風搖曳、發出細碎聲響，散發著一抹空靈而神秘的色彩。

DECEMBER 12, 2025

Lantern Fires of Gion / 祇園古物町家的紙燈初亮

As evening approaches, historic wooden machiya houses light up their traditional paper lanterns, casting a warm amber glow onto the stone-paved pathways. / 當夜幕降臨，歷史悠久的木造町家建築相繼點亮傳統紙燈籠，將溫暖的琥珀色光芒灑在古老平滑的石板路面上，增添古樸美感。
